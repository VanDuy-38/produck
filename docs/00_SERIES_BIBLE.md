# 00 — SERIES BIBLE

> Working title: **BO & YOU** (alternatives: *Being Bo*, *You Are Bo*, *Bo, Again*).
> Market: English-speaking YouTube (US / UK / CA / AU first, global second).
> Style: **flat 2D animation**, thick navy outlines, simple stick-style bodies.

---

## 1. Positioning

| | |
|---|---|
| One-liner | **"Every episode, you're Bo. Every episode, something goes very wrong."** |
| Promise | A short animated movie where *you* are the hero, with real science and survival facts baked into the plot. |
| Audience | 13–24, watches animated storytime/POV channels, Minecraft/Roblox survival content, light science. |
| Formats | **POV** ("POV: You…") · **WHAT IF** ("What If You Could…?") · **HYBRID** (a power + a countdown). See `01_STORY_ENGINE.md`. |
| Length | Long-form 10–14 min (16:9) + 5 Shorts per episode (9:16), cut from pre-planned points. |
| Cadence | 1 long-form per week (target) or every 2 weeks (minimum); 1 Short per day. |
| Rating | PG. Crush storylines stay wholesome (no kissing, no dating talk beyond "I like them"). |

**Why these formats:** in a sample of six uploads from a comparable channel, the three titles that combined *POV + crush or "trapped" + a time limit* earned ~32–37K views. The titles without a crush or the POV framing earned 1K–10K. Six videos is a small sample, so it's a direction to test rather than a proven rule. The `04_EPISODE_LOG.md` metrics will tell us if it holds for us.

---

## 2. Narration rules

- **Second person, always.** The narrator says "you". Bo is the picture of "you" on screen.
- Narrator: dry, deadpan, quick (~150 words/min). Never shouts. Comedy comes from understatement.
- Sentences ≤ 20 words. One idea per sentence.
- US spelling and units first, metric in brackets when a number matters: "‑40 °F (‑40 °C)".
- Bo speaks rarely (1–6 lines per episode), and his lines are short. Other characters speak in dialogue.

## 3. Cast

### BO: "You" (lead)
Model sheet: `art/characters/bo/bo_model_sheet.svg`

| | |
|---|---|
| Age | ~15 (never stated on screen, so everyone can project) |
| Look | Rounded-square head, **yellow headphones (never removed)**, red zip jacket, white sneakers, single-stroke limbs |
| Personality | Overthinker. Kind. Makes terrible plans with total confidence. Brave only when someone else is in danger. |
| Catchphrase | **"Okay. I have a plan."** Said right before the worst decision of the episode (max 2× per episode). |
| Signature move | **Taps headphones twice** before doing something big ("tap-tap" SFX + 1-second music sting). |
| Emotion meter | **Headphone mood light**: a small light on each ear cup changes colour: calm ⚪ · idea 🟢 · panic 🔴 · sad 🔵 · crush 🩷 · focus 🟣. It works at thumbnail size, and the colour can carry a scene with no dialogue. |
| Running gags | (1) The headphones play the **wrong song at the worst moment**. (2) Bo always forgets **one** thing (usually a charger). |

### RAE: the crush (recurring)
Model sheet: `art/characters/rae/rae_model_sheet.svg`
- Identifiers: **high ponytail with an orange scrunchie**, mint-green oversized hoodie (long hem, thick sleeves, kangaroo pocket), side-swept fringe. Palette: hair #5A3B2E · hoodie #7FDDC0 · scrunchie #FF9F43.
- Default face is **unimpressed**; signature pose is **arms crossed**.
- Emotion meter: the **ponytail** (up = alert/scared/laughing · neutral · droop = sad/tired). One rotation/swap around the scrunchie, like Bo's mood light.
- Competent, dry, sarcastic, secretly afraid of the dark. Always slightly better at survival than Bo, which is the joke.
- Arc rule: they get closer every episode but **never "get together" in Season 1**. The "will they?" keeps the comments busy.

### JUNO: best friend
*Design already drafted (tall, lanky, spiky hair, purple tee).*
- Conspiracy-theory enthusiast, loyal, terrible at whispering. Used in "with your best friend" POVs and as comic relief.

### MR. PICKETT: science teacher
- Thick glasses, chalk dust on both sleeves. Hosts the **Lab** frame (F8). Delivers "the science" when the narrator steps aside.

### MOM: mostly off-screen
- Phone always ringing at the wrong moment. "Did you eat?" is her only question.

### THE GREY RAINCOAT: series mystery
- A figure in a long grey raincoat, face never seen, **never affected by the episode's rules** (doesn't freeze when time stops, isn't covered in snow in a blizzard, isn't shrunk…).
- Appears **somewhere in every episode**, sometimes for 6 frames in the background. Viewers hunt for them, and that drives rewatches and comments.
- Every episode reveals exactly **one** new clue. The clue log lives in `04_EPISODE_LOG.md`.

## 4. Visual identity (2D)

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Style | Flat 2D, no gradients on characters, 5 px navy outline (#1F2A44) at 1080p. |
| Characters vs backgrounds | Characters are fully saturated. **Backgrounds are lower-saturation and have no outlines**, so Bo always pops. |
| Bo palette | skin #FFE0C2 · jacket #FF6B6B · headphones #FFC83D · shoes #FFFFFF · blush #FF9AA2 · ink #1F2A44 |
| Accent colours | Danger red #FF4D4D · Day-counter yellow #FFD23F · Night navy #1B2350 |
| On-screen text | **Bold rounded sans** (e.g. *Nunito Black* / *Fredoka Bold*), ALL CAPS, white with 6 px navy stroke. Max 4 words, hold ≥ 1.2 s. |
| Counters | Day / hour counter fixed **top-left**, yellow card, navy text: `DAY 3`, `HOUR 14`, `23:59`. |
| Chapter cards | `RULE 1`, `PROBLEM 2`, `DAY 4` with a tiny headphone icon. |
| Cinema mode | At the story's turn (low point), switch to **2.39:1 letterbox**. The bars signal "this is the movie part now". |

### Titles
- Formula: `POV: You [verb] [with who] [where] [for how long]` or `What If You Could [power]?`
- ≤ 60 characters; put the number in digits ("7 Days", not "seven days").
- Never reuse another channel's exact title or thumbnail composition.

### Thumbnails
- Bo's face ≥ 35% of the frame. Use the expressions **SHOCKED / SCARED / CRUSH / NERVOUS**.
- One threat or object, exaggerated (a wall of snow, a ticking clock, a giant ant).
- ≤ 3 words. The day or number is the hero word (`DAY 7`, `24 HOURS`).
- Bright, contrasting background. Test 2–3 versions with YouTube's thumbnail test.

## 5. Sound identity

| | |
|---|---|
| Logo sting | 2-second "tap-tap → bass drop", used after the cold open. |
| Signature SFX | "tap-tap" (headphone tap) + soft "bwomp". |
| Punchline rule | Music cuts out completely 0.5 s before every punchline. |
| Loudness | −14 LUFS integrated, dialogue ≥ 10 dB above music. |
| Voice | Narrator: one consistent human or AI voice for the whole series. Bo: a separate young voice, used sparingly. |
