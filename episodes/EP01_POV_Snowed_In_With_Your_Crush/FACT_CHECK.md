## 2. Required fixes

### Fix A — Shot 6.8: no cold bottles on a hypothermic chest ⚠️ (safety, QA §4)
The bottles were filled with snow on Day 2 and melted with body heat, so they're **cold**. Putting them on Rae's chest would cool her core — the opposite of the VO, and something a kid could copy.

- **Old visual:** "Takes off his red jacket, wraps her. Bottles on her chest"
- **New visual:** "Takes off his red jacket, wraps her. Pulls her into the fort, gym mats under and around her, sits close against her back"
- VO unchanged: *"You do what you learned on day one. The fort. Body heat. Warm her chest first, not her hands."*
- Optional extra line (if time allows): *"And don't rub her arms. That can make it worse."*

### Fix B — Shot 7.4: cranking math (accuracy + a better joke)
- **Old VO:** *"A minute of cranking buys you a few minutes of power. You crank for an hour."*
- **New VO:** *"A minute of cranking buys you about ten minutes of radio. Charging a walkie-talkie? Way less. [.] You crank for an hour."*
- On-screen: keep `+60 MIN`.

### Fix C — Shot 7.8: where the thermal reading came from
Thermal cameras read surface heat; they can't see people through walls or windows. The firefighters have to have scanned **inside**.
- **Old line:** *[FIREFIGHTER] "Thermal picked up three heat signatures in there. Where's the third kid?"*
- **New line:** *[FIREFIGHTER] "We swept the building with thermal. Three heat signatures. Where's the third kid?"*
- Visual: firefighter coming back out of the doors, thermal camera screen shows 3 warm blobs in the dark gym. (Bonus: the third blob can be a Grey Raincoat frame for pause-hunters.)

### Fix D — STORYBOARD episode card, "Facts" row
"Severe hypothermia: shivering can stop" → "Moderate hypothermia: shivering can stop".

## 3. Optional upgrade (stronger science beat for 4.8)
Physics, no source needed (latent heat of ice ≈ 334 J/g): melting **1 litre** of snow and warming it to body temperature costs ≈ 490 kJ ≈ **117 kcal**. At ~100 W, that's roughly **80 minutes** of your body's entire heat output. On-screen: `1 L SNOW = 80 MIN OF BODY HEAT`.
