## Episode card

| Slot | Content |
|---|---|
| Power | Tap headphones twice → shrink to ~5 mm. Tap twice again → back to normal |
| Cold-open problem | Bo needs his phone, which fell behind the fridge, while his crush waits on the other end of a video call |
| Gag | His headphones shrink with him and keep playing music. Now it's a mosquito whine nobody can find |
| 3 real facts | ✔︎ Atoms are almost entirely empty space · ✔︎ Estimated ~20 quadrillion ants on Earth (PNAS, 2022) · ✔︎ Square–cube law: small animals survive falls that would kill large ones |
| Big question | "If your brain shrinks to the size of a grain of sand… are you still you?" |
| Origin (age 12) | Class presentation panic → tap-tap → hid inside a pencil sharpener for the whole period |
| Rule 1 | Anything you're touching shrinks with you |
| Rule 2 | **Objects** you let go of return to full size after 10 seconds. **People don't**: they stay small until you touch them and tap again |
| Rule 3 | When you grow back, you **take up space**: everything nearby gets shoved away (climax) |
| Montage | Riding a paper plane · Surfing a drop of rain · Sneaking into the movies in a popcorn kernel · Front row at a concert on the singer's mic · Racing a ladybug |
| Problems 1–4 | see below |
| Turn | Juno accidentally gets trapped in a sealed jar while tiny (he grabbed Bo's sleeve, Rule 1) and the air starts to run low |
| Win with | Rule 3: Bo climbs into the jar, grows back, and the jar breaks outward |
| Grey Raincoat | Stands inside Rule 3's shove zone and **doesn't move an inch** |
| Moral Q | "Would you use it to listen in on what your crush says about you?" |
| Mystery Q | "Why didn't the Grey Raincoat move?" |
